Cloudline changes: original GLB bytes and embedded texture are unmodified.
Godot imports the model with generated LODs/shadow mesh; runtime rotates its
+X nose to game +Z. A separate procedural three-strut/six-wheel gear assembly
was added and follows the existing gear visibility state. No third-party
livery or airline marks were added. Flight dynamics and collision dimensions
were not changed. This visual is a game representation of an A320, not a
certified exact A320-216 configuration. Flaps/control surfaces are not rigged
or animated. The other L-4 and J-120 visuals remain procedural placeholders.
